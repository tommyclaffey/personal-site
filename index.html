About

I came to design from running businesses, which is why I care less about how a screen looks in isolation and more about whether an engineer can build it on Monday. A lot of my work is the unglamorous half of design systems: auditing what already exists, finding where two components quietly disagree, and writing the decision down so nobody has to guess.

I'm dyslexic. That's why accessibility isn't a checklist item for me, and why I spend more time on whether an interface is legible than on whether it's pretty.

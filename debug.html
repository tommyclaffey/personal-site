About

I'm Tommy Claffey — a Product and UX Designer based in Metro Detroit with experience spanning user experience design, product strategy, brand development, and creative operations.

My background in content marketing and design gives me a comprehensive understanding of the user journey that informs every design decision, from wireframing and prototyping through high-fidelity delivery.

I approach design as a form of storytelling, blending user-centered thinking with strategic leadership to craft products and experiences that don't just look great, but solve real problems and connect with people in meaningful, lasting ways.

Let's build something worth using.
